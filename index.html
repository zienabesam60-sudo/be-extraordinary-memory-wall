From “I hope I can”

The First Step

I walked into a new environment with excitement, questions and a little fear of the unknown.

Stepping Into the Field

Real conversations, real doctors, real feedback. Every visit pushed me to communicate better and think faster.

Meeting the Real Heroes

I discovered that the internship wasn't only about products or targets. It was about people — the people who challenged me, helped me and believed in me.

Discovering My Superpowers

Rejection stopped feeling like a personal rejection. A difficult day became a lesson. And every small win became proof that I was growing.

Becoming Extraordinary

I learned that being extraordinary isn't about being perfect. It's about showing up, adapting, connecting and refusing to stop.

03 / The people

I came for the internship.
I stayed for the people.

One of the most unexpected parts of this journey was meeting people from different places — from Gharbia to Cairo, Fayoum, Kafr El-Sheikh, Sohag and Minya. Different stories, different personalities, one shared experience.

“I came for the internship.

And that's the part I never want to forget: the people who made the waiting, the pressure, the new places and the long days feel lighter.
